About this inventory

This is a public reference list, not a benchmark or a ranking. Listing a dataset does not mean it is accurate or suitable for a particular purpose. Providers, observations, derived products, reanalyses and models differ, so check each record’s data nature, limitations and terms, then follow its sources.

The hazard groups include climate-related hazards and adjacent snow, ice and landslide resources. Inclusion does not imply that every recorded event was climate-caused. Coverage is uneven: the hail, tornado and thunderstorm wind records are U.S. sources, not global ones. Each record gives its own last-checked date, and terms can change, so confirm them with the provider before use.

To suggest a correction, send whoever shared this page the record name, the text to change and a link to the provider’s documentation.
