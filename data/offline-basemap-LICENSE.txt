Reference map data and attribution

OpenFreeMap • © OpenMapTiles • © OpenStreetMap contributors

The embedded overview and on-demand tile shards are filtered OpenMapTiles
vector data from OpenFreeMap's November 15, 2026 weekly planet release.
The underlying OpenStreetMap replication timestamp is November 9, 2026 UTC.
The package retains selected layers, locally relevant and English names, and
the original geometry of each retained feature. Source range hashes, the
distribution manifest hash and the digest of every tile shard are provided
with the package.

OpenFreeMap's schema uses Natural Earth for generalized small-scale
geography, including low-zoom lakes and rivers. Natural Earth is public
domain. The offline overview is appropriate for geographic orientation.
https://openfreemap.org/
https://openmaptiles.org/docs/schema/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The packaged data and build_offline_basemap.py provide the machine-readable
extract and the instructions to reproduce it. The upstream archive is
published independently of the public OpenFreeMap tile service.
